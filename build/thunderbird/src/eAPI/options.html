How to use with mouse shortcuts

  • Mouse Gestures: Click and hold the Right Mouse Button anywhere inside an open email's viewport, then scroll your Mouse Wheel up or down to change the zoom magnification.
  • Instant Snap: While holding the Right Mouse Button down, click the Mouse Wheel Button (Middle-Click) to trigger your custom Center Button Behavior.
  • System Bounds Sync: This add-on dynamically uses Thunderbird's advanced preferences values, zoom.minPercent and zoom.maxPercent, to set the minimum and maximum allowable zoom.
